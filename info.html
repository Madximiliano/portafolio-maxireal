About

Born in Santiago, Chile, Maximiliano Vásquez is a stylist, artist, and creative consultant who combines his background in advertising and creative management with a keen, almost instinctive aesthetic vision. After six years of working in communications and retail fashion, his approach to the market intersects the commercial and the conceptual, developing recognizable identities for his diverse client portfolio.

His creative career is influenced by the big cities & the cyberculture, a life as a music addict, and the ability to translate sounds into images, developing in-depth research, storytelling that reinterprets symbols with precision, and avant-garde proposals.

His personal work celebrates youth and ingenuity, rescues the uniqueness of the outsider and the sophistication of the underground, inviting us to challenge traditional clothing norms and creating fresh and unforgettable images.
